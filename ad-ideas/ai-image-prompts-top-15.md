# AI Image Prompts for the Top 15 Static Ads (ChatGPT Images, Sunburst), v2

These match the v2 briefs in `static-image-ads-exterior-cleaning.md`. There's one copy-paste prompt per ad, plus exactly what to upload with it.

v2 adds three things to every prompt:
1. **The new copy:** lines that lift the buyer one level and affirm who they are.
2. **A trust strip** of social proof across the bottom of every image.
3. **Mood direction**, so every image feels warm and cared-for (see below).

Sunburst is the slower, high-detail version of ChatGPT Images 2.5 (Flare is the fast one). It handles detail, text and repeat edits better, so use it for finals and Flare for quick drafts, if ChatGPT lets you choose.

Fill in `[brackets]` before you paste. Replace `[brand color]` with your color name or hex code (e.g. "navy #1F3A5F"). The service area is Albemarle County, with most customers in Charlottesville, Crozet and Glenmore.

---

## Three rules built into every v2 prompt

### 1. The mood rule (Map of Consciousness)

Hawkins says people respond to the emotional energy of what's in front of them before they think about it: calm, warm and cared-for draws them in, while gloom and anxiety push them away. So every image should feel **warm, calm and cared-for**, even the problem shots:

- **Warm natural light**, never gray and gloomy.
- **Homes look loved.** The dirty glass is one thing left to do, not a sign of neglect. That keeps shame out of it.
- **Genuine, relaxed faces** and open body language, never stiff or salesy.
- **A fear image** (the ladder) is serious but bright, and the copy lifts it to courage.

### 2. The trust strip (social proof on all 15)

Every prompt ends with the same slim bar across the bottom:

`★★★★★ [#] five-star Google reviews · [Business Name]`

For example: "TideWater Window Cleaning · 47 five-star Google reviews." AI sometimes draws the wrong number of stars, so check it every time.

### 3. Real photo vs. AI photo (unchanged from v1)

| | Ads | What AI does |
|---|---|---|
| **Use your real photo** | 1 The Question · 2 Half-Clean Window · 4 "You remind me of my son" · 5 Reviews · 6 Route Ad · 14 Map Pins | You upload the real photo, screenshot or map. AI only adds the text and layout. |
| **AI makes the image** | 3 Low Sun · 7 Shoes Off · 8 It's Alive · 9 Soft Wash · 10 House Cleaner Gap · 11 WASH ME · 12 Ladder · 13 Walk-Through · 15 The View | AI makes the whole scene. These show a common problem or an idea, not a result you're claiming. |

**Never use AI for:** before/after results, your crew's faces, customers' faces, reviews or your job map. Trust is what your buyers are paying for, so proof has to be real. Facebook can also label images it detects as AI-made.

## Your reference kit (gather once, reuse every time)

| # | What | Used in | Tips |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **3 photos of your face**: straight on, 3/4 angle, smiling | 1, 4 (if AI-made) | Daylight, no sunglasses or hat, plain background |
| R2 | **Your work shirt**, front, logo visible | 1, 4, 9 | Lay it flat or wear it, good light |
| R3 | **Logo**, PNG with transparent background | Any | AI often redraws logos badly. If it does, leave it out and add the real file in Canva |
| R4 | **Your truck**, side view with branding | 6 | Clean truck, daylight |
| R5 | **2–3 homes you've cleaned in Albemarle** | Any AI-made scene, for the local look | Brick colonials and farmhouses in Crozet, Glenmore-style homes, Charlottesville neighborhoods |
| R6 | **Proof files**: half-cleaned window photo, Google review screenshots, map of your jobs, neighborhood signs | 2, 5, 6, 14 | Real ones only, with the customer's OK |
| R7 | **Optional: one past ad that worked for you** | Any | Add the line "Match the layout and text style of the attached ad." |

**When you upload, say what each image is for**, e.g. "Image 1 is my face. Image 2 is my work shirt. Keep my face accurate."

**Size:** 4:5 portrait (1080×1350) for the feed. If ChatGPT gives you another size, ask it to "extend to 4:5 with a plain background" or crop in Canva. **Spelling:** every prompt says "spelled exactly." Still read every word before you run it.

## Follow-up prompts for variations (the Kaleidoscope)

Stay in the same chat after you get an image you like:

- `Make the exact same image with no text at all.` Do this for every winner, so you can test new headlines in Canva in seconds.
- `Same image. Change the headline to: "[new headline]". Keep everything else the same.`
- `Same image. Change "[Crozet]" to "[Glenmore]" everywhere.` That's one version per area.
- `Same image. Make the text bar [red / yellow / white with black text].`
- `Same image. Add a thin [brand color] border around the edge.`
- `Make a 9:16 version for Stories and Reels. Keep the top 15% and bottom 20% free of text.`
- `Same scene, but the house is a [brick colonial / white farmhouse / two-story with divided-pane windows].`

## Last check before any ad goes live

- [ ] Every word is spelled right (read it out loud)
- [ ] The trust strip shows 5 stars and your real review count
- [ ] The logo matches your real logo (if not, add the real file in Canva)
- [ ] Real photos, screenshots and maps weren't changed
- [ ] No made-up results, crew members or customers
- [ ] No extra text the AI added on its own
- [ ] **Mood:** it feels warm and cared-for, not gloomy or shaming
- [ ] **Lift:** the copy moves them up a level (not just scared or guilty) and affirms who they are
- [ ] All the `[brackets]` are filled in with true numbers and real neighborhoods
