# AI Image Prompts for the Top 15 Static Ads (ChatGPT Images, Sunburst)

These go with `static-image-ads-exterior-cleaning.md`. There's one copy-paste prompt per ad, plus exactly what to upload with it.

Sunburst is the slower, high-detail version of ChatGPT Images 2.5 (Flare is the fast one). It handles detail, text and repeat edits better, so use it for finals and Flare for quick drafts, if ChatGPT lets you choose.

Fill in `[brackets]` before you paste. Replace `[brand color]` with your color name or hex code (e.g. "navy #1F3A5F").

---

## Read this first: real photo vs. AI photo

Your buyers hire you because they trust you. Your best ads are proof ads, and proof only works if it's real. So there are two kinds of prompts below:

| | Ads | What AI does |
|---|---|---|
| **Use your real photo** | 1 The Question · 2 Half-Clean Window · 4 "You remind me of my son" · 5 Review Screenshot · 6 Route Ad · 14 Map Pins | You upload the real photo, screenshot or map. AI only adds the text and layout. |
| **AI makes the image** | 3 Low Sun · 7 Shoes Off · 8 It's Alive · 9 Soft Wash · 10 House Cleaner Gap · 11 WASH ME · 12 Ladder · 13 Walk-Through · 15 The View | AI makes the whole scene. These show a common problem or an idea, not a result you're claiming. |

**Never use AI for:** before/after results, your crew's faces, customers' faces, reviews, or your job map. A fake before/after is a false claim (FTC), and if a customer can tell, it kills the trust your whole business runs on. Facebook can also label images it detects as AI-made, which is one more reason to keep proof ads real.

## Your reference kit (gather once, reuse every time)

| # | What | Used in | Tips |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **3 photos of your face**: straight on, 3/4 angle, smiling | 1, 4 (if AI-made) | Daylight, no sunglasses or hat, plain background |
| R2 | **Your work shirt**, front, logo visible | 1, 4, 9 | Lay it flat or wear it, good light |
| R3 | **Logo**, PNG with transparent background | All | AI often redraws logos badly. If it does, leave the logo out and add the real file in Canva |
| R4 | **Your truck**, side view with branding | 6 | Clean truck, daylight |
| R5 | **2–3 real local homes you've cleaned** | Any AI-made scene, for the local "look" | Brick colonials, vinyl two-stories, farmhouses: whatever your area really looks like |
| R6 | **Proof files**: half-cleaned window photo, Google review screenshots, map of your jobs, neighborhood entrance sign | 2, 5, 6, 14 | Real ones only, with the customer's OK |
| R7 | **Optional: one past ad that worked for you** | Any | Add the line "Match the layout and text style of the attached ad." Don't upload other companies' ads to copy |

Brand colors and fonts go in the prompt as words, not uploads.

**When you upload, say what each image is for**, e.g. "Image 1 is my face. Image 2 is my work shirt. Image 3 is my logo. Keep my face and logo accurate."

---

## Rules built into every prompt

- **4:5 portrait (1080×1350)** for the Facebook and Instagram feed. Third-party sites list different size options for this model, so if ChatGPT gives you another size, ask it to "extend to 4:5 with plain background" or crop in Canva.
- **"Looks like an iPhone photo"**, not glossy stock. Raw beats polished (`Proof Checklist`).
- **Exact text in quotes with "spelled exactly"**, and where it goes. Then **check every letter**. Image models still misspell sometimes.
- **"No other text"**, so it doesn't add fake signs, logos or captions.
- **One focus per image, no crowds, faces normal size** (Ogilvy).

# THE 15 PROMPTS

### 1. The Question  ·  Use your real photo (best)

**Shoot:** On your next job, have someone take a phone photo of you in your work shirt, holding a squeegee in front of the house and looking at the camera. Get the homeowner's OK to use the house.
**Upload:** that photo + R3 logo

```
Turn the attached photo into a Facebook feed ad, 4:5 portrait (1080x1350).
Keep the photo exactly as it is. Do not change my face, body, clothes, the house, or the lighting. Do not retouch or smooth anything.
If the photo needs more room for text, add a plain solid [brand color] bar across the top instead of inventing new scenery.
In the top bar, in bold white sans-serif, spelled exactly:
"When's the last time you had your windows washed?"
Bottom left, small white text with a subtle shadow, spelled exactly: "[Business Name] · Charlottesville"
Place the attached logo small in the bottom right corner.
No other text, icons, or watermarks.
```

**No photo yet? AI version.** Upload R1 face photos + R2 shirt + R3 logo + one R5 home:

```
Create a photorealistic 4:5 portrait photo (1080x1350) that looks like a friend took it on an iPhone.
Image 1-3 are photos of me: keep my face and build accurate. Image 4 is my work shirt: I'm wearing it. Image 5 shows the style of homes in my area.
I'm standing in the front yard of a two-story home like the one in image 5, in Charlottesville, Virginia, holding a window squeegee at my side, looking straight at the camera with a friendly, relaxed half-smile. Late-morning natural light, green lawn, mature trees. Behind me, one first-floor window is clearly cleaner and sharper than the others.
Real skin texture, natural light, not glossy, not stock-looking, no studio lighting.
Keep the top third simple (sky and roofline) for text. Add a solid [brand color] bar across the top third with bold white sans-serif text, spelled exactly:
"When's the last time you had your windows washed?"
Bottom left, small white text, spelled exactly: "[Business Name] · Charlottesville"
No other text.
```

### 2. The Half-Clean Window  ·  Real photo required

**Shoot:** On a real job, tape a vertical line down the middle of one outside pane and clean only the left half. Pull the tape, then shoot straight on so the yard shows through both halves.
**Upload:** that photo + R3 logo

```
Turn the attached photo into a 4:5 Facebook ad (1080x1350).
This is a real before-and-after photo. Do not change anything in it: not the glass, the dirt, the clean half, the frame, or the view. No retouching, no sharpening, no cleaning up.
If it needs more room, add plain white space above the photo instead of extending the scene.
At the top, bold black sans-serif on white, spelled exactly: "One window. Half cleaned."
Right under it, smaller, spelled exactly: "Which side looks like yours?"
Add two small labels on the glass in white capital letters with a thin dark outline: "BEFORE" on the dirty half and "AFTER" on the clean half.
Attached logo, small, bottom right. No other text.
```

### 5. The Review Screenshot  ·  Real screenshot required

**Upload:** a real Google review screenshot (with the reviewer's OK) + a real after photo of that home + R3 logo

Image models redraw everything, so they can change the words in a screenshot. **Best method:** use this prompt to make the background and headline only, then drop the real screenshot on top in Canva.

```
Make a 4:5 Facebook ad background (1080x1350) from the attached house photo.
Keep the house exactly as it is, but blur it softly and darken it slightly so text pops.
Top, bold white sans-serif, spelled exactly:
"[#] five-star reviews from Charlottesville homeowners"
Leave a large empty white rounded-corner card in the center (about 80% of the width), with a soft shadow. I'll put a review screenshot inside it myself. Leave the card completely blank.
Attached logo, small, at the bottom center. No other text.
```

If you'd rather let ChatGPT place the screenshot, add: "Place the attached screenshot inside the card. Copy every word, name and star exactly. Do not rewrite anything." Then compare it word for word against the original before you run it.

## Follow-up prompts for variations (the Kaleidoscope)

After you get an image you like, stay in the same chat and use these to make variations fast:

- `Make the exact same image with no text at all.` Do this for every winner. Then you can test new headlines in Canva in seconds.
- `Same image. Change the headline to: "[new headline]". Keep everything else the same.`
- `Same image. Make the text bar [red / yellow / white with black text].`
- `Same image. Add a thin [brand color] border around the edge.`
- `Make a 9:16 version for Stories and Reels. Keep the top 15% and bottom 20% free of text.`
- `Same scene, but the house is a [brick colonial / gray farmhouse / two-story with divided-pane windows].`
- `Same scene in [early spring with pollen / fall leaves / winter with snow].`

## Last check before any ad goes live

- [ ] Every word on the image is spelled right (read it out loud)
- [ ] The logo matches your real logo (if not, remove it and add the real file in Canva)
- [ ] Real photos, screenshots and maps weren't changed
- [ ] No made-up results, crew members or customers
- [ ] No extra text the AI added on its own (signs, labels, fake logos)
- [ ] All the `[brackets]` are filled in with true numbers
