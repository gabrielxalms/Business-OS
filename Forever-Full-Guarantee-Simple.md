# The Forever Full Formula

**I book the jobs. You do the work.**

**How you pay**
- **$997 to start.** It counts toward month 1.
- **The 20%:** 20% of what your customers pay for jobs I book. Not profit.
- **End of month 1:** If 20% is more than $997, pay just the extra. If not, you owe $0.
- **Every month after:** At the end of the month, $997 or 20%, whichever is more.
- **Ads:** I run and pay for your ads. Every Monday, you pay back last week's ad money. No markup. You see the receipt.

**"What if the ads don't work?"**
**First Job in 7 Days, or the Ads Are On Me**
No job booked in your first 7 days of ads? That week's ad money is on me. So is every week after, until I book one.

**"What if I pay and get nothing?"**
**10 Jobs in 30 Days, or It's All Free**
Under 10 jobs booked in your first 30 days of ads? You get your $997 back, plus all 30 days of ad money. Month 1 is free.
*Booked = on your calendar with a deposit. Jobs set for later still count.*

**"What if I don't like you?"**
**Fire Me Anytime**
Any month, any reason, text me "you're fired." You get your most recent payment back, and you owe $0 for that month. You keep your jobs, phone number, customers and website.
One rule: you kept your ads on and showed up to jobs. And once you fire me, your town goes to the next contractor on my waitlist. No coming back.

**"What if I'm never full?"**
**Forever Full or I Work Free**
Not Full in your first 3 months? You pay $0 each month until you are. I keep working.
*Full = tomorrow's jobs booked by 7pm. 1 job a day per crew member, on 4 of every 5 workdays, for a whole month.*
Once you're Full, normal pay starts again.

**Your part**
- Keep your ad budget on: about $150 a day for 1 job a day, $300 a day for 2.
- Show up to every job.
- Send the few photos and videos I ask for each month.

**Only 3 clients. One per town. I only make real money when you get jobs.**
