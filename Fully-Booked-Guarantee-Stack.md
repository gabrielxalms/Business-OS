# The "Fully Booked" Guarantee Stack

A guarantee built from the playbooks in this repo (mainly *$100M Offers* "Enhancing the Offer: Guarantees", *$100M Money Models* "Win Your Money Back", and the guarantee closes in *$100M Closing*).

Fill in the `[brackets]` with your numbers. The worked example assumes you get appointment-based businesses (med spas, salons, clinics, contractors, gyms) booked with clients. Swap in your niche.

---

## 1. The principles it's built on

| Principle (source) | What it means here |
|---|---|
| "The single greatest objection for any product or service is… risk." (*Offers*) | Every layer takes away a different fear: *what if I hate it*, *what if nothing happens fast*, *what if I never get the big result*. |
| A guarantee needs an "or what": *If you don't get X in Y time, we will Z.* (*Offers*) | Every layer below names its X, its Y and its Z. "Guaranteed results" with no penalty is weak. |
| Stack guarantees: an unconditional one up front, conditional ones on top, around **sequential outcomes**. (*Offers*) | 30-day first result → 90-day fully booked → service until they get there. |
| Conditional guarantees should be **"better than money back."** Tie them to the **actions that cause success**. (*Offers*) | The conditions are the same things that get them booked, so the guarantee also improves results. |
| High-ticket or B2B: lean toward **specific, conditional** guarantees. Big unconditional ones are risky when fulfillment costs a lot. (*Offers*) | The unconditional layer is short and only covers your fee. The big promise is conditional. |
| **Service guarantee** ("we keep working free until X") is Hormozi's favorite. It removes time from the equation and almost nobody claims it. (*Offers*) | This is the main promise behind "Fully Booked." |
| **Delayed second payment** and **first outcome** guarantees get both sides focused on the first win. (*Offers*) | Built into Layer 2. |
| **Name it something memorable.** (*Offers*) | Every layer has a name. |
| Do the math. 30% more sales with twice the refunds is still about 1.23x net. (*Offers*) | See section 5. |

## 2. The stack (fill-in template)

### Layer 1: "Keep the Playbook" Guarantee (unconditional, 30 days)
> Work with us for 30 days. If for **any reason** you don't think it was worth it, email us "refund" and we return **100% of our fee**. You **keep everything** we built for you: the ads, the scripts, the follow-up texts, the booking funnel. No forms, no exit call, no hard feelings.

- **Covers:** "What if I just don't like it / it's not a fit?"
- **Why it's safe:** it refunds only your fee, not ad spend, and only for 30 days. The assets you hand over cost you little to duplicate and cost them a lot to build.
- **Optional, stronger:** "…and we'll pay you **$[500]** for your time." (Outsized refund. Only use this if your margins and fulfillment are strong.)

### Layer 2: "First [20] Bookings" Guarantee (conditional, 30 days)
> If you don't get **at least [20] new booked appointments in your first 30 days**, we **don't bill you for month two**, and **we cover your ad spend** until you hit [20].

- Combines the **Delayed Second Payment** and **First Outcome** guarantees.
- **Covers:** "What if it takes forever to work?"
- Hormozi's own example from *Offers*: "I'll guarantee you get 20 people in your first month, or you get your next month free."

### Layer 3: "Fully Booked or We Work Free" Guarantee (conditional, 90 days)
> If your calendar isn't **Fully Booked** within **90 days**, we **keep working for you for free, every month, until it is.** You stop paying us. We don't stop working.

- **Service guarantee** with a defined timeline, which future-paces the result.
- **Covers:** "What if I never get the big result?"
- **Optional, stronger (Personal Service Guarantee):** "…and **[I / the founder] will personally take over your account** until you're full."
- **Optional, outsized:** "…**plus we refund every dollar of ad spend you put in during those 90 days.**"

### Define "Fully Booked" in writing. This is not optional.
A guarantee only has teeth if the outcome can be measured. Example:
> **Fully Booked** = **[90]%** of your **available appointment slots** (based on the hours and providers you give us on day 1) are filled **for [2] weeks in a row**.

Lock in the capacity on day 1 so the goalposts can't move: hours, providers, chairs, trucks.

### The conditions (the work that gets them booked)
They qualify for Layers 2 and 3 if they:
1. **Call or text every new lead within [5] minutes** during business hours (or use our auto-responder).
2. **Keep ad spend at or above $[X]/day**, uninterrupted.
3. **Keep the calendar open** with the slots and hours agreed on day 1.
4. **Use our scripts and follow-up sequence** as given.
5. **Attend the weekly [15]-minute check-in call** (no more than 1 missed).
6. **Give us CRM or calendar access** so both sides can see the numbers.

> "In a perfect world, 100% of your customers would qualify for a conditional guarantee, but will have achieved their result, and therefore will not want to take it." *($100M Offers)*

Present the conditions as **the plan**, not as fine print: "Here's exactly what you do, here's exactly what we do, and if we both do our part and you're not full, it's on us."

### Optional Layer 4: "Win It Back" Bonus (*Money Models*)
> Hit Fully Booked within 90 days **and** send us a video testimonial, and we'll **credit your first month's fee** toward month four.

This makes the guarantee an incentive. They're chasing the result, not a refund, and you get proof for your ads (Danny's client in *Money Models* brought **13 referrals** from one before-and-after).

## 3. Worked example (copy-paste version)

> **The Fully Booked Promise: three guarantees, zero risk.**
>
> **1. Keep the Playbook (30 days).** Give us 30 days. If you don't love it for *any* reason, say the word and get every dollar of our fee back. You keep the ads, scripts and follow-up system we built.
>
> **2. First 20 Bookings (30 days).** If you don't get 20 new booked appointments in your first 30 days, you don't pay us for month two, and we pay your ad spend until you hit 20.
>
> **3. Fully Booked or We Work Free (90 days).** If your calendar isn't 90%+ full for two straight weeks within 90 days, you stop paying and we keep working, free, until it is.
>
> All we ask: answer leads within 5 minutes, keep your ads running, keep your calendar open, and show up to a 15-minute call each week. If you do your part and we don't do ours, it's on us. Not you.

## 4. How to say it on the call (closing scripts)

**Say it boldly and give the reason why** (*Offers*):
> "Do you think we'd still be in business if we gave a guarantee like this and weren't good at what we do? We can make this promise because we've done it for [N] [niche] businesses like yours."

**Best case / worst case** (*Offers* and *Closing*):
> "Best case, in 90 days you're fully booked and thinking about hiring. Worst case, we work for free until you are, and you keep everything we built. Both options are risk-free. The only option *guaranteed* not to get you booked is walking away today."

**Three options close** (*Closing*):
> "Let's look at the options. One: you do this, you get booked, life's great. Two: you do this, it doesn't hit, and we work free until it does. Three: you don't start, and nothing changes. Only one of those has a *guarantee* of not getting you where you want to go. Which one do you want?"

**Fladlien's "fully informed decision" framing** (*Offers*), for the 30-day layer:
> "I'm not asking you to decide forever today. I'm asking you to make a fully informed decision, and you can only do that from the inside. Get in, see if everything I said is true. If it's not, you've got 30 days to tell me and get your money back."

## 5. The math (do this before you launch)

From *$100M Offers*:
```
Without guarantee: 100 sales,  5 refunds (5%)  =  95 net
With guarantee:    130 sales, 13 refunds (10%) = 117 net   → 1.23x
```
Run your own version:
- **Layer 1 exposure** = fee × expected refund rate. Hormozi had **2 claims out of 4,000** on his satisfaction guarantee.
- **Layer 2 exposure** = one month's fee + the ad spend to reach the first [20]. Only promise a number you hit for **8 or more out of 10** clients today.
- **Layer 3 exposure** = your fulfillment cost per extra month (not your price). Hormozi: "I have yet to have a single person say a client took them up on it."

**Rule:** set the guarantee numbers (20 bookings, 90%, 90 days) at what you **reliably** deliver now, not your best case.

## 6. Guardrails

- **Guarantee shoppers make bad clients** (*Offers*). The action-based conditions screen them out.
- **High fulfillment cost?** Keep Layer 1 to your fee only, never ad spend. Make Layers 2 and 3 conditional.
- **Put it in the contract** with the Fully Booked definition, the conditions, and how both sides track them (shared calendar or CRM).
- **Pick the right customer.** If their calendar has no real capacity, or their offer converts badly, the guarantee won't save it. Qualify them on the sales call.
- **Check local consumer and advertising law** for refund and guarantee wording, especially in B2C.

## 7. "Stupid to say no" checklist (Value Equation)

| Lever | How the stack moves it |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome ↑ | "Fully Booked", which they can picture and measure |
| Perceived likelihood ↑ | Specific numbers, deadlines, and a plan written into the conditions |
| Time delay ↓ | The 30-day first result comes before the 90-day full result |
| Effort and sacrifice ↓ | 5-minute replies and a 15-minute weekly call; we do everything else |
| Risk → 0 | Refund + keep the assets → free ad spend → free service until full |
